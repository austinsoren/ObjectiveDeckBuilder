Validation rules

Objective deck requirements

The normal Objective deck contains exactly 20 cards.

General cards cannot outnumber cards unique to the selected crew affiliation.

No more than 10 cards may come from single-card designs.

Every selectable design is treated as a fixed bundle. A card printed in three copies is added as exactly three copies.

Character objectives sit outside the normal 20. Their subtitle and rank must match a model in the crew roster.
